## Regulation 5.1

The Secretary-General shall establish a normal working week and shall for each duty station, after staff consultation, determine official holidays, in addition to those mandated by the General Assembly. Exceptions may be made by the Secretary-General as the needs of the service may require, and staff members shall be required to work beyond the normal work schedule when requested to do so.

## Regulation 5.2

Staff members shall be allowed appropriate annual leave.

## Regulation 5.3

The purpose of home leave is to allow staff members who serve and reside outside of their home country to renew and strengthen their cultural and family ties. Eligible staff members shall be granted home leave once in every 24 months. However, the Secretary-General may grant home leave once in every 12 months to eligible staff members at duty stations having the most difficult conditions of life and work under specific conditions, as approved by the General Assembly.

## Regulation 5.4

Special leave may be authorized under conditions established by the Secretary-General in exceptional cases.
